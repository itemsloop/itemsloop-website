Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 10, 2024

ItemsLoop ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, username, and password when you create an account
  • Item Data: Information about items you add, including names, descriptions, categories, and custom attributes
  • Photos: Images you upload to document your items
  • User-Generated Content: Any other information you choose to provide within the app

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features used, interactions, session duration, crash reports
  • Location Data: Approximate location (if you grant permission) for geolocation-based features

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing and maintaining the ItemsLoop service
  • Syncing your data across multiple devices
  • Improving app functionality and user experience
  • Analyzing usage patterns to optimize performance
  • Communicating with you about app updates and features
  • Detecting and preventing technical issues or abuse
  • Complying with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Data Storage

  • Your data is stored both locally on your device and on secure cloud servers
  • Cloud data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Server location: Primarily in secure data centers complying with international standards

3.2 Security Measures

  • Industry-standard encryption (TLS/SSL) for data transmission
  • Secure authentication mechanisms
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and monitoring

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell your personal information. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

4.1 Service Providers

  • Cloud storage providers (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud) bound by strict confidentiality agreements
  • Analytics services for app performance monitoring

4.2 Legal Requirements

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property
  • To prevent fraud or abuse
  • In connection with law enforcement cooperation (as detailed in our Terms of Use)

4.3 Business Transfers

  • In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may be transferred (you will be notified)

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Access and Control

  • Access: View and export your data at any time through the app
  • Update: Modify or correct your information directly in the app
  • Delete: Request account deletion by contacting us (see Section 10)
  • Data Portability: Export your data in a structured format

5.2 Privacy Settings

  • Manage photo permissions in your device settings
  • Control location access through device settings
  • Opt out of analytics (where technically feasible)

5.3 Regional Rights

If you are in the EU/EEA (GDPR), California (CCPA), or other regions with specific privacy laws, you may have additional rights:

  • Right to be forgotten (data erasure)
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint with supervisory authorities

6. Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: Personal data is deleted within 30 days of account deletion request
  • Legal Holds: Some data may be retained longer if required by law (minimum 6 months in Macau for certain records)
  • Backups: Backup data is purged within 90 days of account deletion

7. Children's Privacy

ItemsLoop is not intended for users under 13 years of age (or higher as required by local laws, such as 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us immediately at itemsloop@gmail.com.

8. Third-Party Services

Our app may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before providing any information.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for EU data transfers
  • Compliance with Privacy Shield principles where applicable
  • Adherence to local data protection regulations

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted with a new "Last Updated" date. Significant changes will be notified via:

  • In-app notification
  • Email (if you provided one)
  • Website announcement at www.itemsloop.com

11. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of Macau Special Administrative Region, subject to mandatory local regulations (e.g., GDPR in the EU).
